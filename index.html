Good to Know

Before you inquire

A few quick
answers.

What types of events do you serve?

Island Bartending Services welcomes inquiries for weddings, birthdays, graduations and other private celebrations.

Where are you based?

Matt is born and raised in Kapolei and serves events on Oʻahu.

Do you offer nonalcoholic drink service?

Yes. Matt offers mocktails as an alcohol-free option alongside cocktail service.

Do you provide the physical bar?

Yes. Matt supplies the physical pop-up bar for the event. Additional inclusions are confirmed in your custom quote.

How is pricing determined?

Quotes are customized based on the event date, location, estimated guest count, service time and bar needs.
